01The Firm

Kendberg is a research-led proprietary trading firm. We build trading systems using mathematics, statistics and data, and we trade only our own capital. We start from Indian markets, with the ambition to become a global research-led trading firm built from India.

India’s research-first white space.

India has talent, liquidity and market depth, but no homegrown research-first proprietary trading institution at global scale yet. Kendberg is being built for that gap.

  1. i.Most visible Indian quant firms focus on high-frequency trading and market making.
  2. ii.Kendberg is building a research-first, medium-frequency model.
  3. iii.Research is the core product and IP of the firm.

03Approach

How we work.

Every strategy passes through the same disciplined research method before a single position is taken.

01/ 04
  1. Step 01

    Generate ideas

    Convert observed market patterns into precise, testable rules.

  2. Step 02

    Test deeply

    Backtest across years of data, many stocks and different market conditions.

  3. Step 03

    Reject weak signals fast

    Only ideas that survive strict testing move forward. Most do not.

  4. Step 04

    Deploy with risk control

    Only risk-controlled strategies go live, with portfolio risk limits and daily monitoring.

Our edge comes from research discipline, not discretionary calls.

    Quant Researcher

    On-site, India · Freshers to 3 years

    Apply→

    Responsibilities

    • Turn observed market patterns into precise, testable trading rules.
    • Backtest ideas across years of data, many stocks and different market conditions.
    • Reject weak signals early and document results in the research database.
    • Work with developers to take validated strategies live under risk limits.

    Skills

    • Strong probability, statistics and linear algebra.
    • Python for data analysis (pandas, NumPy).
    • Understanding of overfitting, out-of-sample testing and backtest pitfalls.
    • Degree in mathematics, statistics, physics, computer science, engineering or similar.
